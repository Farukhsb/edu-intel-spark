# GradeAI Impact Summary

GradeAI was built to help lecturers identify students who may be struggling before problems become serious.

The platform uses assessment evidence to show who may be at risk, why they may be at risk, and what lecturers can do next. It brings together marking, feedback, cohort analytics, risk signals, intervention recommendations, student plans, and follow-up actions in one workflow.

AI-assisted marking is included because it creates structured assessment evidence. It helps lecturers review work faster and more consistently, but it is not the main purpose of the platform. The main purpose is early student support.

## What the platform helps with

- spotting students whose marks are falling
- identifying weak rubric areas and learning outcomes
- highlighting high-risk students before the next submission cycle
- recommending practical lecturer actions
- opening student plans and intervention workflows
- recording support actions and follow-ups

## Why it matters

In many academic settings, warning signs are noticed too late. A student may miss work, receive poor feedback, or fail an assessment before support is properly coordinated.

GradeAI helps move that process earlier. It turns assessment data into support action, so lecturers can intervene before failure, disengagement, or dropout becomes the outcome.

## Evidence in the product

The live platform already shows this through:

- predictive at-risk detection
- risk score summaries
- student achievement trajectories
- weak rubric outcome review
- cohort-level failure signals
- intervention recommendations
- open student plan and contact student actions
- create intervention workflows
- improvement plans and student-facing support

GradeAI is therefore not simply an AI marking tool. It is an academic support system that uses assessment intelligence to help lecturers act earlier.
