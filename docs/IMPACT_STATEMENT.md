# GradeAI Impact Statement

GradeAI was built to help lecturers spot students who may be struggling before it is too late.

Many students show early signs of difficulty through missed submissions, falling marks, repeated feedback issues, or weak performance in specific rubric areas. In practice, these signs can be scattered across different systems and may only be noticed after a student has already failed or disengaged.

GradeAI brings assessment, feedback, analytics, and intervention records into one place. AI-assisted marking is included to make the assessment process faster and more structured, but the main purpose is student support. The platform helps lecturers see who may need help, understand why, and record what support was offered.

The goal is simple: help institutions move from reacting after failure to supporting students earlier.
